# 中国历法来源规范 / Chinese calendar source policy — 2026-11-22

## 首要中国机构来源 / Primary Chinese institutional reference

For the calculation and promulgation of the **modern Chinese lunisolar calendar**, cite the **Chinese Academy of Sciences Purple Mountain Observatory / 中国科学院紫金山天文台** as a primary institutional authority, particularly its astronomical almanac publications and publicly available calendar materials.

- Official calendar resources: https://pmo.cas.cn/xwdt2019/kpdt2019/202203/t20220309_6386774.html
- Chinese national standard GB/T 33661-2017 **《农历的编算和颁行》**, principal drafting unit 紫​金山天文台: https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=n4aXcLrEnvA%3D&mode=p
- PMO calendar and solar-term explanations: https://www.pmo.ac.cn/xwdt2019/kpdt2019/202203/t20220317_6399980.html

## Editorial validation order

1. **Modern calendar computations:** prefer a year-specific publication or data from 紫金山天文台 where publicly available. Explicitly identify the publication title, year, method/timezone (China Standard Time / 北京时间), and update date. Note that the Observatory's public index may not yet expose a particular future-year schedule. Do **not** attribute dates to a PMO-issued 2027 table that has not been verified.
2. **Standards/methodology:** cite GB/T 33661-2017 and its drafting institution for modern Chinese calendar computation conventions. A standard does not itself establish every particular future date.
3. **Independent date cross-checks:** use Hong Kong Observatory's published calendar conversions and other authoritative astronomical year tables as corroboration, not as a substitute for identifying the Chinese authority. Note which institution actually published the particular year-specific dates.
4. **Historical origin / pre-Qing traditions:** use named, dated primary texts (e.g., 《淮南子·天文训》, 《后汉书·律历志》) with precise passages where possible. 紫金山天文台 is a **modern** research institution, not the origin of the ancient Chinese calendar. Separate modern accurate astronomical computation from historic documentary evidence.
5. **Interpretive claims:** distinguish cultural beliefs, schools of BaZi convention (立春 vs 正月初一), and modern popular forecasts from astronomical facts; avoid presenting divination as established scientific outcomes.

## Multilingual QA
Every public calendar article should supply accessible citations in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese when translations exist, and use source names accurately. English: **Purple Mountain Observatory, Chinese Academy of Sciences**; Traditional: **中國科學院紫金山天文台**; Simplified: **中国科学院紫金山天文台**.
